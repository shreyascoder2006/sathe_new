    Same twin.
    Different question.

    HOW IT IS BUILT

    Observation to action,
    in eight steps.

    01

    CCTV, college systems and optional IoT emit events.

    02

    Ingestion normalises them into shared campus entities.

    03

    Computer vision turns video into counts, queues and flow — never identities.

    04

    The twin store holds current state, history and spatial relationships.

    05

    ML services predict demand, detect anomalies and optimise allocation.

    06

    A retrieval layer grounds every answer in campus documents and live state.

    07

    Role-based interfaces show each person only what they are permitted to see.

    08

    Approved actions flow back to people and authorised campus systems.

    FRONTEND

    React / Next.js, Three.js scene for the twin, WebSocket live state.

    BACKEND

    FastAPI or Node, REST plus WebSocket, PostgreSQL with PostGIS, Redis.

    VISION

    YOLO-family detection and tracking on prerecorded feeds for the prototype.

    INTELLIGENCE

    LLM with retrieval and tool calling; time-series baselines for forecasting; constraint solver for allocation.

    RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

    Cameras that count,
    not cameras that watch you.

    A campus platform only earns deployment if the people inside it are comfortable with how it works.

    Counts, not identities

    Zone-level analytics by default. Face recognition is not required for anything the product does.

    Least privilege

    Raw feeds stay with the people whose job needs them. Every role sees a different slice.

    Facts marked apart from guesses

    Live measurement, prediction and recommendation are labelled distinctly, never blended into one confident number.

    Humans approve consequences

    Access changes, emergency controls and shutdowns wait for a person. Every AI-triggered action is logged.
